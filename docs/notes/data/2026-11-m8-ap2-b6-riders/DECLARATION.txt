THE CHANGE UNDER TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RACE (C235) SERIALISE, not refuse. NonLinearProgram::mark_shared_constraint_rows(), called by
    get_mat_space() on every lay before the claims: per constraint block an owner table over the rows
    (the partition that names a row, or "shared" once a second partition names it); every partition copy
    of a constraint function gets unique_constraints_ = !(one of its rows is shared). On one partition the
    flags are reset to true and no table is built. The Jacobian scatter of the raw sites (nlp_adapter,
    fixed_variable_row) and of the local blocks already locks a contested column when
    !unique_constraints_; a cross-partition shared entry's column is contested by construction (both
    partitions claim a slot under it), so its two writers take one mutex. Why serialise: rows summed by
    several applications are a documented builder feature (shared-row overcounts, integrand
    accumulation); refusing them would remove it, and a refusal at the builder would not cover the raw
    route. Unshared declarations (and every Nlp, which lays one partition) keep the lock-free scatter;
    the evaluation code is unchanged, and the layout cost is one pass over the row maps per lay.
    Demonstrated in dev before this declaration (not evidence): with the call disabled, the new
    PiecesSharedEntries pins failed 3 runs of 3 (lost Jacobian updates at repetitions 0, 4 and 13 of 25;
    the 4-partition solve differed); with it, they pass.
