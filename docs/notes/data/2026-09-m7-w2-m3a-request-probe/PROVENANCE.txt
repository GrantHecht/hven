supports no conclusion here. Two budgets exist and are NOT timings:

    callback (RULE.md section 6). `deadline_seconds=300` and `deadline_fired`
    are printed on every leg. `deadline_fired=0` on ALL TWELVE: no leg is
    TRUNCATED, so no leg's status is UNRANKED on that ground.

    would make the leg CENSORED and produce NO ROW. It did not fire on any
    leg: all 12 exited rc=0 (`WRAPPER_RC ... rc=0`, and `LEGS_WORST=0`).

WHAT IS ASSERTED here is counters, statuses and the S5 restoration record --
the deterministic per-process columns -- plus the residual-class floats the
probe prints. Nothing in this artifact is a timing claim.
