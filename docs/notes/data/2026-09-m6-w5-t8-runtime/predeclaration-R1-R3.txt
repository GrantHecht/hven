W5 T8.9r FIX ROUND 1 -- THE TWO RULES FIXED BEFORE THE RE-MEASUREMENT
=====================================================================
Settler rulings R1 and R3, 2026-09-11, in answer to astra's I5 (a) and (e).
This file exists so that "the rule preceded the runs" is a checkable fact and
not a claim: logs/F0-predeclaration.log records this file's sha256 and
comparator.py's, with the UTC time at which both were fixed, and every timed
log of the round carries a LATER FOREGROUND_START. The rules are implemented in
comparator.py (its module docstring states them in the same words) and are
quoted in PROVENANCE.txt's fix1 block.

R1 -- THE VETO'S "REPRODUCIBLE SLOWDOWN" IS THE BANDED READING.
  docs/notes/2026-09-m6-w5-t6-ownership.md 11.1 says "a reproducible slowdown --
  three solo runs, same sign -- on ANY cell of ANY mode on EITHER leg is a veto
  trigger, regardless of the corpus total". That sentence admits a strict
  reading (any cell slower in all three paired rounds, however small) and a
  banded one (a cell OUTSIDE 0.99-1.01, slower in all three). The strict
  reading fires on sampling alone: with 27 cells and a fair coin per round,
  ~3.4 cells are same-sign by chance, and round 1 observed 4 / 3 / 7.
  SAME-SIGN MOVEMENT INSIDE THE BAND IS WHAT "FLAT" MEANS.

  A CELL IS A REPRODUCIBLE SLOWDOWN WHEN ITS MEDIAN RATIO IS OUTSIDE 0.99-1.01
  AND IT IS SLOWER IN ALL THREE ROUNDS.

  The comparator prints BOTH counts, classifies on the banded one, and says so
  on the line it prints. The strict count is reported as informational. The
  ruling is recorded on the ledger at the group-1 close.

R3 -- THE INTERIOR LEG'S FIRST-ROW RULE, PRE-DECLARED BY POSITION.
  The interior leg runs in ONE process and writes its rows in order. THE FIRST
  ROW EACH `--engine interior` PROCESS WRITES IS THAT PROCESS'S WARM-UP -- MKL's
  first call, the allocator's first growth, the page faults for the whole
  working set -- AND IS EXCLUDED FROM THE BAND. It is reported separately with
  its own three paired ratios, and the corpus figure is printed BOTH WAYS:
  WITHOUT it (PRIMARY, by this rule) and WITH it.

  The exclusion keys on the row's POSITION in the raw CSV, not on its name and
  not on its value. Round 1 excluded a row BY NAME AFTER observing that it was
  unstable, which astra's I5 (a) is right to refuse; this rule is decided
  before the round's first sample exists and applies to whatever row the
  process happens to write first. If the arms or the rounds disagree about
  which row came first, that is a MANIFEST failure and the comparator exits
  non-zero rather than choosing.
