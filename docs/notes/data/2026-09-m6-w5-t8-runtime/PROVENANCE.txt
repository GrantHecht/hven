M6 W5 T8.9r -- THE GROUP-1 RUNTIME PROTOCOL: PROVENANCE (CLAUDE.md section 7)
=============================================================================
Read this first: it governs every number in this directory.

WHAT THIS DIRECTORY IS
  The ONE solo runtime measurement of W5 T8's group 1 (T8.1-T8.9). Group 1
  asserts no runtime number anywhere else: every pin in it is a COUNTER pin,
  and the migration guide's group-1 section says so. This is where the runtime
  question is answered, on the recipe and the bands of
  docs/notes/2026-09-m6-w5-t6-ownership.md section 11 (bands 11.1, the
  disposition table 11.1.1, LAYOUT-vs-WORK 11.2, the two legs 11.3), with the
  T6 close reading (docs/notes/2026-08-m6-ledger.md) as the table's shape.

  IT IS A MEASUREMENT, NOT A DISPOSITION. Where a band reads MOVED or
  UNRESOLVED, or where a veto trigger fires, this directory reports it with its
  numbers and stops. Section 11.5 and the owner decide what follows.

THE BUILD RECIPE, IDENTICAL ON ALL THREE ARMS
  cmake -S <arm> -B <arm>/build -G Ninja
        -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release
        -DCMAKE_CXX_COMPILER=/usr/bin/clang++
        -DCMAKE_C_COMPILER=/usr/bin/clang
        -DHVEN_BUILD_TESTS=OFF -DHVEN_BUILD_BENCH=ON -DHVEN_A4_GATE=OFF
  cmake --build <arm>/build --target hven_sqp_corpus -j 8

  HVEN_FP_MODE is left at its default, SAFER_FAST -- the same value T6's arms
  passed explicitly. HVEN_BUILD_TESTS=OFF is the ONE recipe difference from
  T6's arms; it changes no compile flag on the library or on bench (the
  uniform regime is set at the top level), and it removes a gtest FetchContent
  that would need the network in a scratch tree. The libhven.a hash agreeing
  with T6's is the evidence that it changed nothing that matters here.

  Submodules: "git archive" carries no submodule content, so dep/eigen
  (bc3b39870ecb690a623a3f49149a358b95c5781d) and dep/fmt
  (407c905e45ad75fc29bf0f9bb7c5c2fd3475976f) were extracted from the
  superproject's own checkouts at those commits. All three arms name the SAME
  two commits -- checked against each arm's tree, not assumed.

THE SOLO RULE, AS DECLARED (CLAUDE.md section 7)
  This leg ASSERTS wall clock, so it ran ALONE. Every timed leg:
    * one solve at a time, one process;
    * "taskset -c 2", with the SMT sibling cpu10 left idle;
    * MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 and OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 in every process;
    * held under "flock /tmp/box-build.lock" for the WHOLE alternating
      sequence -- the lock is never released between the A and the B arm of a
      round, nor between rounds;
    * "pgrep -af 'cmake|ninja|ctest|hven_|codex|clang'" run SEPARATELY before
      the lock was taken, again INSIDE the lock, and again between every
      alternation. Every one of those captures is in the leg's log, verbatim,
      with its UTC timestamp.
  NO CO-RUN PROTOCOL WAS USED and none is declared: nothing here was measured
  under contention. Every wall number here is therefore quotable; no number
  from any co-run appears anywhere in this directory.

  WHAT THE pgrep CAPTURES CONTAIN, so a reader is not misled by a non-empty
  list. They are never empty, and none of what they show is load:
    * the driving shell itself -- the agent's own "bash -c" wrapper, which
      matches "codex" on its exported environment variables, not on any
      running Codex work;
    * "earlyoom", whose own command line lists "clang" among the processes it

  Nothing that BUILDS or SOLVES appeared at any check. No foreign process was

THE LEGS, AND WHAT EACH ONE MEASURES
  LEG 1 -- the U0 27-cell corpus (docs/notes/data/2026-08-m6-w1-acceptance/
  u0-replay-cells.txt line 3), "--engine walk|ssn|ipm", 3x alternating
  A/B/A/B/A/B; per-cell MEDIAN of the three runs per arm; corpus = the SUM of
  the per-cell medians, never a mean of ratios. This is the WALL-CLOCK leg:
  its cells are seconds-scale and its "wall_s" column brackets the SOLVE alone
  (bench/corpus_cells.h, "timed_row" -- the KKT gate sits deliberately outside
  that window).
  Pass A and pass B ran on THREE designated cells per mode --
  f7_n1000_bound_neutral, f7_n5000_bound_neutral, f7_n20000_bound_neutral --
  each as ONE process through "--internal-run-one", so the counts are that
  cell's own setup and solve and not the fork/exec parent's. T6 measured one
  perf cell per mode; this is three per mode, spanning a 20x size range.

  LEG 2 -- the 27 Hock-Schittkowski problems (tests/sqp/support/hs_problems.h)
  driven through the SQP solver by "--hs --repeat N", in BOTH trace variants
  ("--hs-trace off" and "--hs-trace sink"), kept separately identifiable as
  11.3 requires rather than averaged together. N is the calibrated count; see
  calibration.csv.

  THE INTERIOR LEG -- "--engine interior", b9848bf -> e51a7e0. Runs in
  process, with no fork, so a perf capture counts exactly the leg.

THE INTERIOR LEG'S BASE ARM, AND WHY IT IS b9848bf
  The interior leg DOES NOT EXIST at 102f729: T8.1 created it and T8.9 rewrote
  it. So it has no arm at the SQP legs' base, and the honest base is the FIRST
  head at which the leg exists with the same row keys -- the T8.9 close's
  predecessor b9848bf (the T8.8 code head), whose leg carries 41 rows. The
  head's leg carries 43: the two "parts2" rows are new at T8.9, have no base,
  and are reported head-only, informational, with NO band. The 41 row keys are
  the join.

  WHAT THAT ARM MEASURES is therefore T8.9's harness rewrite (wrapper ->
  direct construction) and nothing else. Three terms of T6 section 11 do not
  transfer to it, and the T8.9 brief's A7 said so before any number was taken:
   (i)   the "identical harness SOURCE" term cannot hold -- the head harness
         does not compile against the base library. EACH ARM RUNS ITS OWN
         HARNESS SOURCE, and both executables' sha256 are retained above. What
         saves the comparison is that "wall_s" brackets the SOLVE only
         (transcription precedes t0); the route equivalence rests on T8.9's
         41-row identity pin, not on this leg.
   (ii)  "--repeat" is HS-only (bench/bench_corpus.cpp). The HS071 and
         infeasible rows are millisecond-scale and carry NO wall band; they
         are listed with their ratios and nothing is claimed from them. The F7
         rows are seconds-scale and carry the band under leg-1 rules.
   (iii) the warm rows time the measured solve only, and the callback lever is
         interior-only.

  THE HARNESS-PER-ARM QUESTION FOR THE THREE SQP ARMS, ANSWERED SEPARATELY.
  bench/bench_corpus.cpp DID change between 102f729 and e51a7e0 (+327 lines),
  but every hunk is outside the timed window: the file banner, the includes,
  the using-declarations, the usage text, "write_outcome" and "parse_status"
  (both post-solve CSV code), the HsRow status type, and the interior leg's
  own dispatch. The timed window is bench/corpus_cells.h "timed_row", which
  brackets "solve_target()" alone; inside it the only change is that the
  fourth argument of "driver.solve" became a SolveBudget aggregate -- which is
  the library API under measurement, not a change of measurement method.

WHAT THIS LEG DOES NOT MEASURE, SAID PLAINLY
  THE TOP-LEVEL IPM's RUNTIME ACROSS T8.1-T8.8 HAS NO BASE ARM AND IS NOT
  MEASURED HERE. The interior leg reaches back only to b9848bf. The "ipm" arm
  of leg 1 is the SQP corpus's interior-point QP tier, NOT the top-level
  interior-point solver. What covers T8.1-T8.8 on the top-level IPM is T8.1's
  coverage rule and the identity replays, for CORRECTNESS; its RUNTIME is what
  the M7 benchmark suite will measure.
