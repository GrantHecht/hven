(F1) THE TWO RULES FIXED BEFORE THE RE-MEASUREMENT, VERBATIM IN
     predeclaration-R1-R3.txt (sha256 281ca5a87718581cc0522c0b2d22b563da4b81e53c90eb86bb4d2b0b955feb92)
     and implemented in comparator.py's docstring in the same words:

  R1  A CELL IS A REPRODUCIBLE SLOWDOWN -- 11.1's veto trigger -- WHEN ITS
      MEDIAN RATIO IS OUTSIDE 0.99-1.01 AND IT IS SLOWER IN ALL THREE ROUNDS.
      Same-sign movement INSIDE the band is what FLAT means; a fair coin gives
      ~3.4 same-sign cells of 27, and round 1 observed 4/3/7. The comparator
      prints BOTH counts, classifies on the BANDED one, and says so; the
      strict count is informational.

  R3  THE FIRST ROW EACH "--engine interior" PROCESS WRITES IS THAT PROCESS'S
      WARM-UP (MKL's first call, the allocator's first growth, the page faults
      for the working set) AND IS EXCLUDED FROM THE BAND BY POSITION -- not by
      name, not after looking at its value. It is reported separately with its
      three paired ratios, and the corpus figure is printed BOTH ways: WITHOUT
      it (PRIMARY) and WITH it. If the arms or rounds disagree about which row
      came first, the comparator exits non-zero rather than choosing.

     logs/F0-predeclaration.log records both files' sha256 with the UTC time at
     which they were fixed. EVERY timed log of this round carries a LATER
     FOREGROUND_START, which is what makes "the rule preceded the runs" a
