W5 T8.9r-attrib4 -- THE SCORING RULES, FIXED BEFORE THE FIRST TIMED SAMPLE
==========================================================================

These are the brief's rules, written down here and hashed before this leg's
first timed run exists. Nothing below is decided after looking at a number.

R3 (INHERITED, POSITIONAL). The first row every `--engine interior` process
writes is EXCLUDED from every corpus figure and every per-row table, BY
POSITION, not by name. The cell order is fixed by the measurement pin so that
row is the SAME row at every arm: `f7_n1000_bound_physics/MakeParameter`.
Eleven rows are scored.

THE ARMS, and the pairing. Four arms measured in ONE set of rounds so all four
are paired inside every batch:
   a1 base___  102f729   the interior leg's base
   a2 head___  e51a7e0   the group-1 head
   a3 e1_____  e51a7e0 + experiment-E1.patch
   a4 e2_____  e51a7e0 + experiment-E2.patch
FIVE rounds, one batch per round, arm order ROTATED by (round-1).

THE PER-ROW STATISTIC is the MEDIAN of the five rounds' wall_s for that row at
that arm. The corpus figure is the SUM over the eleven scored rows of those
medians.

RECOVERY, per the brief, per row and per corpus:
    RECOVERY(E) = 1 - (E/base - 1) / (head/base - 1)
where base = a1's figure, head = a2's figure, E = the experiment arm's. It is
reported per row and as the MEDIAN over the eleven scored rows, and separately
on the corpus figure. A recovery is reported with its sign; a NEGATIVE recovery
(the experiment arm slower than the head) is reported as such and is not
clipped.

E3 RUNS ONLY IF the better of E1 and E2 recovers LESS THAN 80 % of the corpus
step. If it runs it is: `perf record -e page-faults -g --call-graph dwarf` on
e51a7e0 for one large cell, reported as a site list by user stack; and
`perf stat -e dTLB-load-misses,dTLB-store-misses,dTLB-loads,L1-dcache-load-misses`
on a culprit-vs-parent pair. At most ONE further experiment, and only if a site
is obvious.

THE CORRECTNESS GATE, for every patched arm, decided before it is run: the
whole 43-row `--engine interior` CSV at the patched arm must be IDENTICAL to
e51a7e0's in every column except `wall_s`. Any single differing cell fails the
gate and the arm's wall figures are reported as UNGATED. `hven_tests` is NOT
built at any arm (-DHVEN_BUILD_TESTS=OFF), so no unit suite is claimed here.

THE RECIPE CALIBRATION, decided before it is run: an unpatched e51a7e0 built by
this leg's recipe must reproduce `attribution/arms.txt`'s libhven.a sha256
(60bfe03f79078eb8ab51fd099a7253f91e232714a008a52be0a40e49403fa30a). If it does
not, this leg STOPS and reports that instead.

THE SOLO DISCIPLINE. Everything asserting wall clock runs under the T8.9r fix1
R2 discipline: one solve at a time, `taskset -c 2`, MKL_NUM_THREADS=1,
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1, the box lock held for the whole batch, the driving shell
pinned off cpu2 and cpu10. THE IDLE ACCOUNTING IS CORRECTED per astra's item 7
(`.superpowers/w5-t8-9r-fix1-review-astra.md`): foreign task time counts
`user + nice + steal + guest` on BOTH cpu2 and cpu10 -- not `user` alone -- with
only the measured run's OWN user time subtracted on cpu2, and pids present in
one snapshot of a batch but not the other are reported as TRANSIENTS rather
than silently dropped. A batch whose window fails the 0.5 % bar on either core
is RE-RUN; nothing is ever signalled.

THE FAULT PASS is separately declared as CO-RUN TOLERANT: `perf stat -e
page-faults,minor-faults` asserts a deterministic per-process counter, not wall
clock, and its wall column is not quoted.
