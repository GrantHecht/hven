READ THE TWO RIGHT-HAND COLUMNS AGAINST THE TWO LEFT ONES. experiment-1 puts every
member from `acceptance_` down back at the PARENT's byte offset, exactly; experiment-2
puts every one of them at the CULPRIT's, exactly. That is what the two patches were
sized for, and it is why their wall results are a test of this hypothesis and not of
some nearby one. `nlp_` is the one member that cannot match to the byte: it is a
16-byte shared_ptr at the parent and an 8-byte raw pointer at the culprit, which is a
T8.4 change of type, not of placement.

Every shift above is a whole number of 64-byte cache lines (320 = 5 lines), so no
member changes its position WITHIN its line and no two members change their distance
apart. That is a fact about the hypothesis, recorded here because it bears on how much
a reader should have expected from it in the first place.
