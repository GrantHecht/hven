W5 T8.9r-attrib3 -- THE SCORING RULES, FIXED BEFORE THE FIRST TIMED SAMPLE EXISTS
=================================================================================
Written at the UTC stamp logged beside its sha256 in logs/B0-predeclaration.log,
which is BEFORE the first FOREGROUND_START of the first wall batch (logs/W*).

R3 (inherited verbatim from T8.9r fix1 ruling R3, as attribution-interior applied
it).  THE FIRST ROW EVERY `--engine interior` PROCESS WRITES IS EXCLUDED FROM EVERY
SCORE, BY POSITION.  The cell order in the pin was chosen so that row is the SAME
row at every arm -- `f7_n1000_bound_physics/MakeParameter` -- which the untimed
smoke (logs/B2-smoke.log) verified at all six arms before any timed sample existed.
It is the process's warm-up: it pays MKL's first call, the allocator's first growth
and the first page-in, and at the earlier legs its own three runs of it spanned
1.66x.  Its steps ARE printed, marked EXCL, so a reader can see why.

THE SCORED SET.  The ELEVEN rows that remain of the twelve base F7 rows after R3.
They occupy row positions 2..12 at ALL SIX ARMS (smoke, verified): the two rows the
T8.4 bench commit eb8f157 adds (`.../solve_optimize`) are written at positions 20
and 21, AFTER every scored row, so no scored row runs in a different position in
its process at any arm.  The seven unconditional rows (13..19) are in every process
at every arm and are not scored.

THE STATISTIC.  Per row, per arm: the MEDIAN of five rounds of `wall_s`.  The step
into an arm is that arm's median over its parent's median.  The cumulative is
b06's median over b01's.  A step is called MOVED on a row when it is outside
0.99-1.01, the band leg 1 and the interior leg both use.

THE CULPRIT RULE, FIXED NOW.  The commit that carries the step is the one whose
median share of the cumulative, in log space (ln step / ln cumulative), is the
largest, AND the leg calls a commit the carrier of "at least 80 %" only if that
median share is >= 0.80.  If two commits split it, both are named with their
shares and no single carrier is declared.

ROUND STRUCTURE.  Five rounds; one round = one batch under the box lock; the arm
order is ROTATED by (round-1) mod 6 so no arm keeps a fixed position in a batch.
