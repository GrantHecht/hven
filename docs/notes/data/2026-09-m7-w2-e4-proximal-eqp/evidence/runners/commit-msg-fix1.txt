E4-11 THE UNREGULARIZED POLISH AND THE TERMINAL POLISH. The proximal answer
carries an O(delta*||x* - x_k||) bias away from the face's exact KKT point, and
that bias is a real error even though delta is a real term. polish_eqp_core
(eliminated) and polish_bordered_unregularized (border) take at most two further
refinement steps against the face's OWN exact system on the factor the solve
already paid for, each kept only while that residual STRICTLY FALLS and only
while the step is a POLISH (kEqpPolishStepCap, calibrated on the corpus: without
it three base-certified cells are lost, at 1e-4 one, at 1e-6 none). On a flat
face the guard rejects the first step and the proximal answer stands. At every
CERTIFIED exit the final point is then POLISHED on its final working set:
stage 1 adopts the pass's own already-polished answer for free, stage 2 re-solves
the face with the dual regularization out of the SYSTEM -- one factorization,
charged to terminal_polish_solves and deliberately NOT to factorizations, which
is the active-set search's cost that every budget and the hot-handle contract are
written against. Adopted only when feasibility is kept, no KKT residual worsens
and one strictly improves. refine_on_face needs nothing of its own: its promise
of the face's exact answer is what the EQP-site polish now delivers.
