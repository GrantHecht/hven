qp(m7): W2 E4 -- the proximal EQP with the walk's tests in its currency, and the EQP-site residual check

THE SYSTEM (E4-1). The EQP is solved from the iterate it is called at: the primal
regularization becomes the proximal penalty (delta/2)||x_F - x_k,F||^2, so the answer
is a descent direction for the face's model by construction and its stationarity
residual carries -delta*p, zero in the limit, instead of -delta*x_F. K is unchanged;
the free block's rhs gains +delta*x_k,F and the refinement backs out only the DUAL
regularization, because the primal one is now a term of the model. `x_k` is a
REQUIRED argument of solve_eqp / solve_bordered_eqp, threaded explicitly through
every function on the path, and the border twin carries the same term on every
variable row -- a pinned row's delta*x_i is cancelled by its pin, which is what the
eliminated path's elimination implies for it.

THE THREE TESTS THAT HAD TO MOVE WITH IT.
 * TERMINATION (E4-2): a pass is a KKT point of its working set when the free-block
   stationarity residual is within opt_tol of its own scale AND no row or bound
   carries a structural violation -- the two tests the classification already decides
   kOptimal on. The zero-motion reading ||p|| <= step_tol is kept as the observation
   it always was and no longer certifies alone: p is now the STEP and carries 1/delta
   on a flat face.
 * THE RUNAWAY GUARD (E4-3): `is_runaway` is asked on every MOVING pass, not only at
   the terminal classification, which is what the three runaway fixtures need once
   the ridge's answer is re-proposed from every point it reaches. They pass
   UNMODIFIED. A ridge-scale guard on the STEP was built, measured and removed: it
   fires on an ordinary degenerate first step (HS10/HS27, alpha = 0 with p_inf 8e9)
   and cost two kOptimal exits. A NON-FINITE answer is refused instead.
 * THE EQP-SITE RESIDUAL CHECK (E4-4): the answer's own miss against the REGULARIZED
   system, over the CONSTRAINT rows, against a scale that carries the dual
   regularization's footprint; over tolerance it buys at most
   kEqpResidualRefineIters solves against that same system and, failing that, is
   REFUSED -- the border path retreating to the eliminated one and the walk taking
   4b's suspect-stall escalation rather than a step. Counters eqp_refine_iters and
   eqp_residual_refusals.
