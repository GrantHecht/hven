THE POST-HOC RE-GATE, AND WHY IT CHANGES NO VERDICT ABOVE
---------------------------------------------------------

so it differs on every neutrality leg for a reason that is not about the walk --
the same defect AMENDMENT 1 was written to fix for the `arm`/`arm_*` keys, in a
field AMENDMENT 1 did not name.  It was found AFTER the chain ran.  RULE.md is
not edited; the transcripts above are the gate that ran and stand.

OPT-IN `--exclude-label trace_out`, and the exclusion is printed with both
values on every leg:

    L   READING6 REPRODUCES  SMBANK REPRODUCES  ZAMB2-8 DIFFERS

reproduce; each arm still fails at least one, so the formal outcome at the top
of this file is unchanged by the defect.  gates/default-unchanged.txt proves the
flag is opt-in: the amended gate, re-run at its DEFAULTS with the chain's own
arguments, reproduces all three committed transcripts and the OFF-control
