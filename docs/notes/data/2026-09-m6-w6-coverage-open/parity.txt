CORRECTIONS -- T0 FIX ROUND 1 (2026-09-13)
==========================================
The sol review of this artifact (FIX-ROUND, no Critical) found two defects in
the caveat above; the settler accepted both (K1, K2). Nothing was re-measured:
no build, no run, no new profile. Every figure below is recomputed from

CORRECTION 1 -- THE DIRECTION OF THE CAVEAT: +1.17 IS A CEILING, NOT A FLOOR.
  The original said "the +1.17 is, if anything, a floor". That is backwards.
  Correcting an UNDER-READ raises the total it lands in. The effect lands on
  HEADERS, and the `src/drivers/` row is fourteen `.cpp` TUs -- it contains no
  header lines at all (`include/hven/drivers/` is a SEPARATE row, reported
  beside it under A10, and is not part of the 8586/9687). So a read without the
  effect moves the LIBRARY total UP and leaves `src/drivers/` where it is: the
  +1.17 is an UPPER BOUND on the lead.

  What that costs the finding, quantified on the DEMONSTRATED defect only.
  The four demonstrated under-read headers miss, between them, 63 lines --
  47 + 9 + 6 + 1, recomputed from `coverage-summary.txt`:
      timer.h                    0/47   (coverage-summary.txt :45)
      eval_error_log.h           0/9    (:34)
      inertia_regularization.h   0/6    (:17)
      ipm_solver_types.h         0/1    (:72)
  Fill EVERY line of all four and the library total moves from
  22259/25448 = 87.4686 % to 22322/25448 = 87.7161 %, i.e. 87.72 %, against
  `src/drivers/` 8586/9687 = 88.6343 %. PARITY STILL HOLDS on the demonstrated
  defect: the lead narrows, it does not invert.

  The WIDER effect -- llvm-cov's "952 functions have mismatched data" across the
  whole library -- is NOT quantified here, and this artifact makes NO directional
  claim about it. What it claims is what it measured: parity is met on the read
  as taken (plan J.1's same-read rule), with that residual uncertainty named.
  This is the reason no number in this directory is a pin.

CORRECTION 2 -- FOUR DEMONSTRATED UNDER-READ HEADERS, NOT FIVE.
  The original paragraph said `zero-headers.txt` "demonstrates five separate
  header files reporting 0 % while their functions execute"; the closing
  paragraph said "five of the eight 0 % headers are not gaps at all". The
  demonstrated count is FOUR: `timer.h`, `inertia_regularization.h`,
  `eval_error_log.h`, `ipm_solver_types.h` -- each with a covered call site and
  an execution count in README.md section 3 and in `zero-headers.txt`. The fifth
  file that was being counted, `soc.h`, is the opposite case: it is GENUINELY
  COLD, corroborated at its call site (`ipm_solver_globalization.cpp:1361` reads
  0), and it is the one real T2 target on the list. The remaining three
  (`math_functions.h` dead, `aggregate_arity.h` compile-time only,
  `super_scalar_traits.h` Eigen-dispatch only) are judged in `zero-headers.txt`
