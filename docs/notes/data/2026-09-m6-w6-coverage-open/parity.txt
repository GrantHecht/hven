THE CAVEAT THAT BELONGS NEXT TO THE NUMBER
------------------------------------------
The library total is itself UNDER-READ, and by more than `src/drivers/` is.
llvm-cov warned "952 functions have mismatched data" on this read, and
`zero-headers.txt` demonstrates five separate header files reporting 0 % while
their functions execute hundreds or thousands of times. That effect lands on
HEADERS (inline definitions), and headers are a larger share of the library
total than of `src/drivers/` -- which is fourteen `.cpp` TUs. So the +1.17 is, if
anything, a floor: a read without the effect would move the library total up and
`src/drivers/` up by less. The parity finding survives the caveat in DIRECTION,
and the caveat is the reason no number here is a pin. See README.md section 3.
