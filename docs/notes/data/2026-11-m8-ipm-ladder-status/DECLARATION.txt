THE CHANGE (78af82e2d)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
After factor_impl, kkt_sol_.info() != Success sets kkt_no_numerics: stop_detail_ = "the inertia-correction ladder at
iteration <i> ended on a failed KKT factorization (numerical issue|backend error); no step can be computed"; the
governor is not run; the step solve and dense guard are skipped; DXSL is zeroed, alpha = 0, GoodStep = false (no line
search, no recovery chain, no exhausted-inertia dispatch); at the loop tail ExitCode = kNumericalError and
last_stop_reason_ = kNone (decisive over the !GoodStep kDiverging override), and the existing terminal block records
the row and returns (return_best substitution as on every other kNumericalError exit). The dense guard's failed re-run
sets the same flag with its own detail ("the dense step guard's refactorization at iteration <i> failed (...)").
STATUS CHOICE: kNumericalError with stop reason kNone and the message carrying what failed -- the init path's exact
convention (a failed initial factorization), and the status singular_abort (SINGULAR_KKT, Ipopt
Error_In_Step_Computation) already uses for an inertia-correction failure. No new status or stop reason.
Docs: result_message.cpp's kNone comment, core/errors.h's exception contract line, docs/testing.md's injector consumers.
