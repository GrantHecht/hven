Question      Which exception types can the adapter throw from an EVALUATION callback, and which of them
              mean "the model cannot be evaluated at this point"? After B5 the interior-point engine
              absorbs hven::solvers::EvaluationError ONLY (it used to absorb every exception at a trial
              point), so an evaluation failure thrown as any other type now ends the solve by propagating.

FINDINGS -- lanes/cutest/cutest_model.cpp (the CUTEst lane's hven model)
  F1  check_status() (cutest_model.cpp:51-57) throws std::invalid_argument for EVERY non-zero CUTEst
      status: 1 = allocation, 2 = array bound, 3 = EVALUATION ERROR. It is called after every
      evaluation call (cutest_cofg in eval_f / eval_grad_f, cutest_ccfsg in eval_g / eval_jac,
      cutest_csh / cutest_cshc in eval_hess).
      Status 3 is CUTEst's own "cannot evaluate here" and is the one case that must become
      hven::solvers::EvaluationError. Statuses 1 and 2 are setup/contract failures and stay
      std::invalid_argument.
      Effect without the change: on the interior-point engine a status-3 failure at a trial point,
      absorbed as a rejected trial before B5, now propagates out of the solve; on the SQP engine it
      propagated before and still does. With the change both engines reject the trial (the SQP gains
      it).
  F2  The size and structure checks in the same file (eval_grad_f / eval_g / eval_jac / eval_hess
      "sized N for M", a Jacobian or Hessian entry outside the declared structure) are contract
      violations: std::invalid_argument is right and stays.
  F3  Non-finite values computed by the SIF functions themselves (a log of a negative, an overflow)
      are returned as values, not thrown. After B5 they are failed trials on BOTH engines (before B5
      the interior-point engine accepted a -Inf merit); no adapter change is needed for them.
  F4  lanes/hven/nlp_model_triplet_view.cpp: every throw is a size/structure contract check
      (std::invalid_argument) -- stays. sets/hs/*: declaration-time invalid_argument only.
      lanes/ipopt, lanes/snopt, lanes/external: not hven-engine callbacks; out of scope.
