THE CHANGE UNDER TEST (AP-1 §4.6 and §13 B5 with the 1ec52f56 amendment; B4-review carries I3, I4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C1  core/errors.h: hven::solvers::Error : std::runtime_error; EvaluationError, BackendError : Error. The
    exception contract stated there and on both solve entries.
C2  IPM: the seven trial-point catch sites absorb EvaluationError only (the declared narrowing). Every
    trial evaluator screens its objective/constraint values (and, on ls_lang, the first-order blocks);
    the soft-feasibility trial screens its full evaluation. A non-finite trial throws EvaluationError
    internally and takes the rejection path.
C3  IPM: the committed point's evaluation (objective, gradient, KKT matrix values, right-hand side) is
    screened every iteration. Non-finite, or an EvaluationError, at a point a step reached: the step is
    rolled back (previous iterate and bound multipliers restored) and the next step capped at
    alpha_prev*cap_prev/backtrack_divisor (compute_step's two halves with the direction scaled between
    them), bounded below by backtrack_divisor^-line_search_max_backtracks; no rollback possible -> the
    phase ends kNumericalError, IpmStopReason::kNonFiniteStart (point never moved) or kNonFiniteIterate.
    An EvaluationError at the solve's start point propagates. The "line search failed ... could not be
    evaluated" std::runtime_error becomes kNumericalError with IpmStopReason::kEvaluationFailure.
    IpmResult::nonfinite_step_rollbacks counts rollbacks.
C4  SQP: EvaluationError at every trial evaluation (trial, SOC point, failure step, box projection,
    restoration candidate and exit point, the regularized-restart point) and at derivative refreshes and
    the exact Hessian becomes an un-evaluable bundle the existing rejection/rollback machinery handles;
    at the start point of a caller's solve it propagates. A non-finite values bundle carries f = NaN
    (a -Inf inequality row has violation 0 and was judged on f alone and could be ACCEPTED: pre-existing
    defect, fixed). No SOC on a non-finite trial (it fed infinite data to the dense factor, which threw
    std::runtime_error out of the solve: pre-existing defect, fixed).
C5  SqpStopReason (11 values) published at every exit, SqpSolver::last_stop_reason(); IpmStopReason gains
    kNonFiniteStart, kNonFiniteIterate, kEvaluationFailure, kException (7-10). Result messages name the
    reason on both engines (kNumericalError causes, kInterrupted callback vs exception, the two uncertified
    kInfeasible exits with a reason); a restoration sub-solve's own reason travels in the message.
C6  Foreign exceptions: recorder guards (std::uncaught_exceptions) in alg_impl, run_phase_sequence and
    solve_impl_body keep the interrupted solve; the handles finish it (row space, work, message) into
    last_result() (new on both handles), status kInterrupted, reason kException, and rethrow the same
    object (I4). last_result() also holds every returned result; cleared at each solve.
C7  EvaluationErrors moves to SolveResult (both engines): rejected evaluations, thrown or non-finite.
C8  thread_pool.h: a dispatch rethrows ONE captured exception unchanged, preferring a non-EvaluationError
    (was: a composed std::runtime_error when several workers threw).
C9  BackendError at the backend boundaries: the MKL/Accelerate adapters' analyze and solve_partial
    (session runtime_errors re-thrown as BackendError, message unchanged; the analyze fault injector
    throws BackendError), KktFactor::factorize_checked, DenseSymmetricFactor LAPACK dsytrf/dsytrs info.
    Session files untouched. No iparm field touched.
C10 docs/trace-schema-v0.md: ipm.phase.exit stop_reason lists every value (work_limit/time_limit were
    missing; the four new ones added).
Notes in this directory: HVEN-BENCH-AUDIT.txt (the obligation-before-landing audit; the change it
proposes is hven-bench's, made when its pin moves), TYCHO-CATCH-UP.txt (registered catch-up, not a gate).
