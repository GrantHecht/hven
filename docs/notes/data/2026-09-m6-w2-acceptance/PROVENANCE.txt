THE SERIAL RULE, AS DECLARED (CLAUDE.md section 7)
  Every measurement leg ran SOLO -- one process alone on the machine, one leg
  at a time, each leg held under the box build lock (`flock /tmp/box-build.lock`)
  with `pgrep` verified clean before it started. Measurement legs (the six
  replay legs, the A4 gate, the two kIpm counter legs, the U0 reachability
  probe) additionally ran pinned with `taskset -c 2` at MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 and
  OMP_NUM_THREADS=1. The SMT sibling of the pinned core (cpu10) was left idle
  and measured from /proc/stat across each window:

      six-leg U0 replay window ......................  0.1251 % busy
      replay window including the A4 gate ...........  0.1150 % busy
      U0 reachability probe window ..................  0.0379 % busy

  NO WALL-CLOCK NUMBER IN THIS DIRECTORY IS ASSERTED. Counters, statuses and
  residual columns are the asserted currency; every `wall_s` column and every
  suite/leg duration is informational and is excluded from every comparison
  below (75 of the corpus schema's 76 columns are compared; 65 of the A4
  arm's 66).

  Coverage ran in its OWN instrument tree (build-coverage, the
  linux-clang-coverage preset) serially under the same lock. Coverage flags
  change codegen: nothing from that tree is a pin, a baseline or a timing.
