THE CHANGE
- KktFactorization::arm(): the FactorArm of the last factorization (recorded in record() and
  record_failed_factorization(); kSparse after reconfigure()/release()).
- After the IPM step solve, when arm() == kDense: rr = ||K x - rhs|| / ||rhs|| on the KKT buffer (upper triangle,
  selfadjointView, member scratch). rr > kDenseResidualRatioSingular = 1e-5 (Ipopt's published residual_ratio_singular
  value; Waechter & Biegler 2006 sec. 3.1; Ipopt options reference; no Ipopt source read) or non-finite -> the system is
  treated as singular: the ladder's additive shifts are removed from the buffer and factor_impl re-runs with
  base_singular (no base attempt; delta_c engaged by the existing rule when available, latching; the ladder entered at
  max(Hpert0, inertia_correction_initial, increase * last total delta_w), then increase * total on a repeat), the
  solve repeated; at most kDenseSingularAttempts = 3; an exhausted re-run ladder ends the loop and the existing
  exhausted path takes it; after 3 the solution is used as it stands (the pre-guard behaviour). Hpert0, h_facs,
  h_pert, h_pert_cum and (proximal mode) rho_k take the re-run's values.
- After the least-squares multiplier solve (init_impl), when arm() == kDense and rr > 1e-5: delta_c =
  dual_regularization(mu) once (its (1,1) block is the identity: only the constraint rows can be deficient),
  refactorize, re-solve; still above (or a failed refactorization) -> the estimate is discarded (equality multipliers
  stay zero; Waechter & Biegler 2006 sec. 3.6).
- Solve sites NOT guarded: the PROBE predictor solve (non-default barrier mode; the same factorization the step guard
  then checks; its output only selects mu) and the SOC correction solves (back-substitutions on the factorization the
  step guard already accepted or repaired).
- IpmResult::dense_residual_ratio_triggers (appended last): one per residual-ratio trigger, both sites. No trace key,
  event, option or printed string changes. The threshold and the attempt count are documented constants
  (detail/globalization/inertia_regularization.h), not options.
