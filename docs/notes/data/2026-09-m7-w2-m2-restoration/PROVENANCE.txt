THE BOX PROTOCOL EVERY LEG RAN UNDER
------------------------------------
ONE CELL PER PROCESS, SERIAL, ALONE. Every measurement leg and every build ran
inside `flock /tmp/box-build.lock` with NO TIMEOUT and NO FALLBACK, with
FOREGROUND_START/END markers printed INSIDE the lock and `set -o pipefail` in
every wrapper. `MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `taskset -c 2` on every
solver leg.

A `pgrep -x hven_bench_cell` guard ran INSIDE the lock before every solver leg
and would have aborted the chain had a foreign cell binary been alive -- which
would mean something was running outside the lock. IT NEVER FIRED, in any chain.

THE OTHER LANE. The harness lane worked the same box throughout, first its A4
sweep and then its 1800 s re-pin tier, taking the lock for a whole cell at a
time. The two interleaved leg by leg; the long chains here are SEGMENTED into
several lock acquisitions for exactly that reason, so a single hour-long hold
would not starve it. No process of this task's was ever signalled by anything
but its own `timeout`, and no foreign process was signalled at all.

WALL IS INFORMATIONAL (CLAUDE.md section 7) and no wall number here is a
measurement. The ladder legs' walls are recorded in the chain transcripts for
scheduling only. The 900 s per-leg cap is a SAFETY STOP, not a DNF condition:
none of these seven cells is a wall cell in H3's run-A, so a leg that hit it
would be a finding, reported as CENSORED and never as a row. NO LEG HIT IT --
all fourteen ladder legs and all six control legs returned rc=0.

WHAT IS ASSERTED AND WHAT IS NOT
--------------------------------
ASSERTED: statuses, counters -- the five S5 fields included -- and the four
declared residuals, all at MKL_NUM_THREADS=1, every leg alone under the lock.
The caller-model `f` and `h` columns of the ladder are the PROBE's own
recomputation from the model after the solve, not engine output, and are labelled
as such in every table.

NOT ASSERTED: every wall-clock number, anywhere.

THE INSTRUMENT'S NEUTRALITY IS PROVED, NOT PRESUMED (neutrality.txt): (i) the
same binary with `--callback on` and `--callback off`, 33 columns x 7 cells,
0 differing; (ii) ZAMB2-8 against H3 run-A, 13/13 identical; (iii) the other six
against M1's committed budget arm, 16/16 identical each.

THE PROBE'S CONFIGURE LINE, for reproduction from this tree:

    cmake -S <hven> -B rel -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release \
      -DCMAKE_CXX_COMPILER=/usr/bin/clang++ -DCMAKE_INSTALL_PREFIX=install \
      -DHVEN_BUILD_TESTS=OFF -DHVEN_BUILD_BENCH=OFF
    cmake --build rel -j $(nproc) && cmake --install rel
    rm -rf probebuild        # hven_DIR is a cache variable find_package reuses
    cmake -S wrappers -B probebuild -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release \
      -DCMAKE_CXX_COMPILER=/usr/bin/clang++ -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH=install
    cmake --build probebuild --target m2_probe -j $(nproc)
    cd <hven-bench> && MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 taskset -c 2 \
      probebuild/m2_probe --problem <CELL> --callback on

(`probe-CMakeLists.txt` is the file named `CMakeLists.txt` in that source
directory; it is renamed here only so the artifact's wrappers/ can hold it
beside the scripts.) THE HARNESS ROOT MUST BE THE CWD -- the CUTEst bridge finds
a decoded problem at `.scratch/h3/decoded/<PROBLEM>`, relative to it.
