WHY THE INTERIOR READING CHANGES. The event removes the very spin the r5 filter exists to subtract: MKL's dsecnd()
clock calibration behind ensure_solver_initialized(), reached only by the interior-point driver. r5's interior guard
requires the EXCLUDED samples of BOTH arms to be > 0 ("filter liveness"); arm B no longer has them by construction, so
the unchanged guard would VOID a correct leg. analyze_f1.py replaces that guard, interior mode only, by three checks
and changes nothing else (stop condition, validity, filter, rounds, band, aggregation are r5's verbatim):
  F1-a  arm A's excluded samples over all its processes and rounds > 0 (the filter is still live: arm A carries the
        spin); else VOID (exit 4), as r5.
  F1-b  arm B's SPIN samples -- symbols difftime, difftime@plt, mkl_serv_get_clocks_frequency, __vdso_time, time, the
        calibration's own and a subset of the exclusion list -- are 0 in EVERY arm B process: the spin is gone and did
        not move to a later call (clock_gettime-class samples, which std::chrono produces, stay excluded and are
        reported, not judged).
  F1-c  all-samples (unfiltered) B/A <= 0.995: the declared improvement is visible. Expected ~0.96-0.97 (PF-1's arm A
        interior: 17,692 excluded of 514,571 samples per round-median; the phase-aligned spin is ~1 s).
  The FILTERED B/A must be in [0.995, 1.005] as before: everything but the spin is unchanged. mkl_get_max_threads(),
  which replaces dsecnd(), costs no measurable instructions (diagnosis/mkl_probe_nolate.txt: 6.08e6 per probe process
  with or without it) -- it moves the OpenMP runtime's first-call bring-up, which every arm A process also pays inside
  its first Pardiso call, to before the solve's timer.
  walk / ssn / ipm (the SQP modes) never reach ensure_solver_initialized(): their reading is r5's, unchanged (excluded
  count reported, expected 0; band; counter identity as the STOP condition).
