WHAT THESE LEGS ARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Re-runs of the hven-bench `hven-sqp` lane's CUTEst TRAIN cells at a tightened
MAJOR-ITERATION CAP. `SolveBudget::max_iterations` can only tighten the engine's
own option (hven drivers/solve_result.h), so `--budget-iters k` produces the
PREFIX of the uncapped solve and the row it prints is that solve's state after k
units of budget — and the budget is shared with the restoration phase, "the
bounded quantity is major_iters + restoration_iters"
(include/hven/drivers/sqp_solver_types.h:250-253), so a ladder over k walks the
restoration phase too. That is the whole instrument: no hven code was built,
changed or even compiled for this page.

NO hven SOURCE, TEST, SCRIPT OR CMAKE FILE MOVES WITH THIS ARTIFACT. The commit
that lands it carries this directory and the design page and nothing else.

CONCURRENCY — MIXED, AND THE SPLIT IS PER LEG (docs/measurement.md §3.1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PART THAT WAS WRONG, FIRST. Every leg here ran while the W2 A4-0 options
sweep was working the same box, holding /tmp/box-build.lock for whole legs of its
own. This task's wrapper (scripts/run_cell.sh) takes that lock where it can and,
after a 20 s wait, PROCEEDS WITHOUT IT. That fallback was not this task's to
make: the sweep's cells run under a 300 s WALL ceiling, and a wall-ceilinged row
is exactly what CLAUDE.md §7 says "must run solo, or co-run only with their full
budget honored". The fallback is recorded here rather than removed from the
record, and the settler has directed the sweep lane to re-run whatever
overlapped.

(raw/NOSUCHPROB-default-b1.log:4 and raw/smoke-SMBANK-b5.log:4). It is on the

would make the artifact differ from what ran, which is the one property it
exists to have; the two lines are named here instead, so the check's output is
explained rather than silenced. No line this task AUTHORED carries trailing
whitespace.
