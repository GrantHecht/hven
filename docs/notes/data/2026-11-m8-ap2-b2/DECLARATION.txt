THE CHANGE UNDER TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Public: model/nlp.h (Nlp, NlpDimensions), model/problem.h (Problem,
  ProblemStatistics), core/types.h gains IndexVecRef and kInfinity.  IpmSolver and
  SqpSolver solve a `Problem &`; their NonLinearProgram / NlpModel / NlpModelAssembly
  overloads leave the public classes.  SolveResult reports `lambda` and `g` in the
  problem's own row space; lambda_e, lambda_i, ce, ci leave its public surface
  (the engines' native split stays reachable through detail/ only).
* To detail/: NlpModel, ParametricNlpModel, NonLinearProgram (and the master-list
  make_nlp), NlpProblemModel, NlpModelAssembly, make_nlp_program.  NlpTripletModel is
  replaced by Nlp.  starting_multipliers retired.
* Entries for tests and bench: detail::problem_from_program, detail::problem_from_model
  (and detail::problem_from_assembly for the SQP's bridge route).  Each forwards to the
  engine's existing internal entry unchanged.
* Per solve on a Problem(Nlp): dimensions(), both structures and bounds() re-read;
  per-iterate caches cleared; an unchanged bound set is a no-op; a changed set re-lays
  the transcription; a dimension or structure change, or a row-kind flip, is refused
  by name.  A pieces Problem (Problem(AssemblyDeclaration)) is refused by name on the
  SQP (owner decision 8).
* The 9 B2 lines of scripts/export_contract_detail_includes.allow are deleted.

RUNTIME NEUTRALITY (CLAUDE.md section 5 / docs/measurement.md section 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B2-R1 (the band).  Arms: A = the pin's hven_sqp_corpus (build-b2/armA, sha256
      0ba5d824...), B = B2's hven_sqp_corpus.  Default (internal) route.  The B1 protocol
      verbatim: the 27 cells, one process per cell via --internal-run-one for walk, ssn,
      ipm; --engine interior whole; perf stat -e instructions:u,branches:u,cycles:u,
      branch-misses:u,L1-icache-load-misses:u; three rounds, arms alternated per cell
      within a round; one untimed unrecorded warm-up per arm per batch; taskset -c 2
      (SMT sibling cpu10 left idle, not proven by /proc/stat), MKL_NUM_THREADS=
      OMP_NUM_THREADS=1; per arm the per-cell MEDIAN of the three runs; corpus = SUM of
      per-cell medians.  BAND: corpus instructions B/A in 0.995-1.005 in every mode.
      STOP condition: compare_replay.py on each (round, cell) pair's two CSV rows; a
      counter difference is not an instruction measurement.  Pass B (Zen 3 front-end
      events) is EXPLANATORY, taken only if pass A leaves the band.
B2-R2 (informational, no band).  The public route's per-solve re-read cost: B2's binary,
      nlp-public vs nlp-internal, one round, walk and interior, same perf events; the
      ratio is reported, never gated (the two arms do different setup work by design:
      the internal arm re-transcribes per solve).
Wall-clock is carried beside the instructions and is INFORMATIONAL.  Every leg runs one
process at a time, alone, under flock /tmp/box-build.lock with pgrep checked empty before
and inside the lock.
