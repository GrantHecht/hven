WHAT THIS LEG IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE HEAD measured against ITSELF: two ARMS of the same binary differing only by
`SqpOptions::elastic_rung_engine` (kWalk, the shipped default, and kTier). It is
not a commit pair. The question it answers is the owner's, registered at M6 W2
Q-O2 and carried into M7 as M7-1 / D11: does the elastic ladder's rung A cost
grow with problem size on the WALK, and does an interior-point rung fix that?

THE CELL, AND WHY IT HAD TO BE BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
No natural F7 population in hven fires the elastic rung at all — 0 of 1200 cells
in the M6 W2 T7 reachability sweep, 0 of 27 in the M6 close-gate replay, all
three arms. The cells that DO fire are three HS problems (embargoed by M7's
TRAIN rule until W1 H2) and four constructed W2 fixtures, the largest n = 2.
So the vehicle is constructed: `F7InconsistentChain`
(tests/sqp/support/elastic_rung_vehicle.h) = F7CollocationChain(N, 3 states,
2 controls) plus ONE scaled contradictory equality pair (s = 1e2, b = 1) over
the last node's control block. Identical, constant Jacobian rows; residuals
differing by 2sb at every point; so the LINEARIZED subproblem is inconsistent at
every iterate by construction and the tier's §6.3 detector suspects it.

THE BUDGET, AND WHAT ONE MAJOR DOES AND DOES NOT BUY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
max_iter = 1: ONE major per cell, one tier escape and one PRIMARY fallback entry,
from the same start point at every size — bounded (CLAUDE.md §8) and
like-for-like across sizes. An unbounded run does not finish N = 100 in 540 s
and would price the SOLVE rather than the RUNG.

It is NOT "one ladder per cell" on both arms, and an earlier version of this file
said it was (sol's review, Minor 5; corrected 2026-09-16). The WALK arm is one
ladder: `elastic_activations = 1`, six rungs, five of them escalations. The TIER
arm is three activations and five rungs — three of its five rungs DECLINE, so the
driver re-enters — with `elastic_floor_retries = 1`, `ipqp_fallback_rung_b = 1`
and `elastic_from_ipqp_escape = 2` at every size. The raw log carries all of
those columns per cell.
