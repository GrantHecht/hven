THE CHANGE
- SymmetricFactor::Options::dense_condition_check (bool, default false; refused under kNever). Under kAlways/kAuto, after
  dsytrf and the block walk accept a factorization, the dense session estimates its reciprocal condition number with
  LAPACK dsycon (1-norm; ||A||_1 from the stored upper CSR; workspace sized at analyze()). rcond < 2^-53
  (kDenseConditionSingular: LAPACK xSYSVX's test, RCOND < DLAMCH('Epsilon'), "singular to working precision"; LAPACK
  documentation only) or NaN -> the factorization is refused and the platform session retakes it within the same
  factorize() through the existing exactly-singular fallback (evidence, status and arm kSparse are the platform's).
  Counters::dense_condition_fallback_count (appended last) counts these; each also counts in dense_fallback_count, so
  factorize_count = dense_factorize_count + dense_fallback_count + sparse-selected factorizations still holds. A
  platform-session handle carries the flag to its adopter. The Accelerate shim gains LAPACKE_dsycon_work.
- SQP wiring: sqp_kkt_options(threads, dense) sets the check iff dense != kNever (every KktFactor: the walk's K0 /
  BorderState, the eliminated EQP factors, refine_on_face's and polish's temporaries, the SSN); the IPQP tiers set it at
  reconfigure (dense policy only); the rank repair's Gram factor keeps it off (its shift t = 1e-6*omega bounds rcond
  near 1e-6, and its inverse iteration solves with a near-singular G on purpose). The IPM never sets it (its DN-1b guard
  stands).
- Counting: QpCounters::dense_condition_fallbacks and SqpCounters::dense_condition_fallbacks (appended last; trace: one
  additive key, the two golden lines re-derived), SsnResult / IpqpResult::dense_condition_fallbacks; fed by factor
  counter deltas at the walk's four factorization sites and the SSN's two, and by the IPQP's counter delta; folded
  beside eqp_polish_steps at every SQP fold (6 + 3 + 4 sites) and beside factorizations in the SSN/IPQP conversions and
  charges.
- DN-1b riders: F1 the step guard's re-solve runs only when the re-run left numerics (info() == Success), else the loop
  ends; F2 the init guard's failed refactor records result_.last_kkt_info; F4 factor_dc_applied_ removed, factor_impl's
  out-parameter (threaded through dense_step_singularity_guard); F5 the strip comment; the PROBE predictor solve's
  unguarded note; DenseArmEngines.IpmResidualGuardRecoversANumericallySingularDenseFactorProximal.

THE MEASUREMENT THE DESIGN WAS CHOSEN FROM (before this declaration; scratch build-dn1c/, not evidence)
- Probe: a scratch copy of the base (dense_session.cpp, env-gated, never committed) recording per dense factorization
  rcond (dsycon), the residual ratio of a fixed +-1 rhs, the min pivot ratio, and per solve ||K x - b||/||b|| split into
  LARGE solves (||b|| >= 1e-6 x the largest ||b|| on that factorization) and SMALL (refinement-like) ones; the 119 SQP
  cells at dense-default: 98,356 dense factorizations, 2,775,708 solves (the DN-1b probe's totals, reproduced).
  * IPOPT's 1e-5 on the actual solves is NOT supported for the SQP: 14,167 factorizations (14 %) carry a LARGE solve
    above it, in 14 cells, 7 of them optimal on both arms with no harm and no catastrophic solve (HS74 38, HS75 31,
    HS84 19, HS101 14, CRESC100 38, BT2 4, DEMBO7 2 factorizations); SMALL (refinement-like) solves above it: 12,473
    solves, in CRESC4 and ACOPP30 (optimal) among others.
  * The catastrophic class (a solve with ratio > 0.1) is 105 factorizations in MESH, MSS1 and HS90 only (ratios to
    4e67); every benign high-ratio factorization stays <= 3.7e-2 (SPANHYD), a gap of > 2.7x.
  * rcond < 2^-53 flags 174 factorizations in 5 cells: all 105 catastrophic ones (largest catastrophic rcond 8.8e-18),
    plus 70 whose actual solves were accurate: MESH 25, ALLINITC 21, SPANHYD 24 (all kNever non-success cells). No
    kNever success cell other than HS90 (its one catastrophic factorization) has one. The next decade (< 1e-15) adds
    HS92 20, LAKES 6, HS99EXP 1 and 285 more SPANHYD.
  * rcond does not separate ALLINITC's accurate factorizations (rcond down to 2e-35) from the catastrophic ones (HS90
    5.6e-35): only a rhs test could, and no rhs exists at factorize(), where the remedy keeps the inertia and the
    numerics from one backend. Rejected: the 1e-5 actual-solve test (unsupported, above); a solve-time remedy (solve()
    is const and the consumer has already read the dense inertia); the min-pivot ratio (not scale-invariant, design
    sec. 1.4); a random-rhs residual test (an invented threshold).
  * Backtraces of every flagged factorization: all at KktFactor sites (solve_eqp under eliminated_candidate:
    reread_wrong_inertia, saddle_direction, refine_terminal_mu_free, polish_terminal_point, border paths; rebuild_k0).
    None in the SSN, the IPQP tiers or the Gram factor.
