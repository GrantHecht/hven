produced -- RULE.md section 4, and repeated in wrappers/chainB.sh's header)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
NO CO-RUN. Every one of the 35 legs ran ALONE: one cell per process, serial,
`MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1`, `taskset -c 2` (one physical core), each
ARM holding `/tmp/box-build.lock` with no timeout and no fallback for its own
seven legs and releasing between arms so the harness lane could interleave. A
`pgrep -x hven_bench_cell` guard ran INSIDE the lock before every leg and would
have aborted the arm; it never fired (logs/chainB.log).

WALL IS INFORMATIONAL. The per-leg `wall_s` in logs/chainB.log is printed
"(INFORMATIONAL)" and is quoted as a measurement nowhere. The 900 s per-leg
`timeout` is a SAFETY STOP whose firing would be reported as CENSORED and never
as a row; it did not fire on any leg (all 35 rc=0).

WHAT IS ASSERTED here is counters, statuses and the S5 record -- the
deterministic per-process columns -- plus `prox_rel`, which the probe computes
from the callback's own x. Nothing in this artifact is a timing claim.
