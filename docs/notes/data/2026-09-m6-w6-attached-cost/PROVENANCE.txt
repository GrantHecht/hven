WHAT THIS LEG IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
It is ONE HEAD measured against ITSELF: ATTACHED vs UNATTACHED, the same binary
in every arm, the arms differing only by a lever.  It is NOT a commit pair.
There is nothing here to keep or revert and no veto to fire — every number is a
FINDING, reported against the ±0.5 % corpus bar (plan §1 W6.T6, §0 J.5 as
amended by A5).

The registration it discharges: `docs/notes/2026-08-m6-ledger.md:4455–4457`,
inside the T8.9r runtime reading — "T8.6/T8.7/T8.7b flat because the corpus
attaches no callback and no sink — their attached cost is UNMEASURED, registered
for W6", and "the HS leg MOVED FASTER in five of six combinations (0.975–0.993),
informational, below its own 0.22 % instrument floor".

THE SERIAL / SOLO TERMS (CLAUDE.md §7)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every run of every leg, wall-asserting or not:

  * ONE SOLVE AT A TIME, alone on the box.  A `pgrep -af
    'cmake|ninja|ctest|hven_|bench_corpus'` is taken in a SEPARATE command
    before the lock and AGAIN inside it; both land verbatim in the batch's
    retained log as PGREP_EMPTY / PGREP_NONEMPTY.  Every batch of this leg
    recorded PGREP_EMPTY on both.  No process was declared-skipped: no
    reviewer launcher shell matched the pattern at any point of this task.
  * `flock /tmp/box-build.lock` held across the whole batch, with
    FOREGROUND_START / FOREGROUND_END written INSIDE the lock.
  * MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 in every measured process.
  * `taskset -c 2` on the measured process; its SMT sibling cpu10 is left idle
    and is MEASURED, not assumed.  The driving shell is pinned OFF both
    (HARNESS_MASK = 0,1,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,11,12,13,14,15), so the scaffold — `ps`,
    `awk`, the timestamps, perf's own setup — cannot land on the reserved core.
  * `set -o pipefail` in every leg script, and every wrapper worst-folds: the
    leg's exit status is the WORST status any step returned and a later success
    never clears it.  Proven against stubbed failures INSIDE the lock:
    logs/03-fold-proof.log (seven wrappers, every one non-zero) and
    logs/03b-run_leg-failure-proof.log (the outer wrapper itself).
  * Nothing of this leg is written under /tmp except the box lock — with ONE
    declared exception that is the harness's and not this task's: the SQP corpus
    leg's fork/exec deadline writes its per-cell row marker at
    /tmp/hven_sqp_corpus_internal_<pid>_<cell>.row (bench_corpus.cpp:~1560) and
    removes it.  That is the shipped runner's own behaviour at every capture
    this project has taken.

THE TWO POPULATIONS, AND WHY THERE ARE TWO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) THE INSTRUCTION READING runs each cell as ONE PROCESS, via
    `--internal-run-one <cell> --engine <mode>`.  This is T8.9r's leg-1 perf
    shape and the reason is the same: the public SQP corpus leg forks and execs
    a child per cell, so a `perf stat` over the leg would count 27 forks, 27
    execs and a parent poll loop beside the solve.  Three rounds; within a
    round the four arms run BACK TO BACK ON EACH CELL, so an attached and an
    unattached reading of the same cell are adjacent in time.

(2) THE WALL READING runs the public 27-cell leg, one arm per batch, one
    reading per arm, and its CSV is also the counter byte-identity population:
    27 cells x the 76-column schema x 3 modes x 4 arms.

THE BATCH SPLIT, DECLARED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A batch is one lock acquisition and is the unit the idle proof is computed over.
The walk-mode instruction rounds were split by CELL GROUP purely to keep a
single foreground command inside this session's 600 s cap; EVERY GROUP IS A
COMPLETE FOUR-ARM ALTERNATION FOR EVERY CELL IT HOLDS, which is what the reading
pairs on.  Round 1 ran groups 1–9 / 10–18 / 19–27; rounds 2 and 3 ran
1–9 / 10–18 / 19–22 / 23–27, after round 1's third group measured 13.5 min.  The
split is a batching device and changes no pairing.

TWO INTERIOR BATCHES, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Batch 1's ROUND-1 UNATTACHED process read +2.1 % instructions over its own
rounds 2 and 3, while all six processes of the batch wrote BYTE-IDENTICAL rows
(`scripts/compare_replay.py`, 43 cells x 30 columns, 0 differences, every pair).
It is therefore a first-process effect, not a trajectory — the same class the
ownership doc §11.3 names ("run 1 is a first-run allocator/page-cache outlier
that a median of three discards").  The median of three already discards it.
Batch 2 re-takes the leg behind ONE UNTIMED, UNRECORDED warm-up process so that
no round is the batch's first; each arm is then internally stable to 0.27 %
(off) and 0.04 % (on).  NEITHER BATCH IS DISCARDED: both are in `perf/` and
`raw/`, and `reading.md` states both.

    alone — `sqpwall-ssn-a00`, cpu2 0.0557 %, cpu10 0.7242 % (0.13 s of foreign
    task time on cpu10 across a 17.95 s window).  It is named here rather than
    re-run: it is the UNATTACHED arm, and sibling contention can only make an
    arm look SLOWER, so it biases the ssn wall ratios TOWARD the attached arms
    and away from the finding.  Every ssn wall ratio is below 1.0 anyway.
    THE INTERIOR AND HS WALL BATCHES are both inside the fraction bar on both
    cores — `ipmleg-wall` cpu2 0.0000 % / cpu10 0.1265 %, `hs-wall` cpu2
    0.1096 % / cpu10 0.0548 % — and are UNPROVEN-EVIDENCE for the same
    snapshot-cadence reason as the instruction batches below (1 and 5 missing
    alternation snapshots): each runs several arms inside one batch, and the
    scripts snapshot per round rather than between consecutive runs.

  THE INSTRUCTION BATCHES are UNPROVEN-EVIDENCE across the board and several are
  UNPROVEN-FRACTION.  Both are DISCLOSED rather than hidden, and neither touches
  the asserted reading:
    * EVIDENCE: the leg scripts snapshot once per CELL (after that cell's four
      arms) rather than between every consecutive timed run, so the tool reports
      "N alternation snapshot(s) missing" for each.  It is a gap in the RECORD.
    * FRACTION: on the short walk groups the whole batch is 2.8 s of timed wall
      and a SINGLE 10 ms /proc/stat tick is 0.36 % of it; 0.15–0.18 s of foreign
      task time reads as 5–6 %.  The tool's own resolution note says exactly
      this.  The long batches at the same recipe read 0.020–0.032 %.
    * Two batches saw a foreign process in state `R` with no re-snapshot:
      `sqpperf-ssn-r1-gall` and `sqpperf-walk-r3-g23-27`.  The second is the
      larger: pid 16979 accrued 46.7 s of CPU across that batch's 806 s window —
      on OTHER cores; cpu2 foreign was 0.025 % and cpu10 0.1125 %.
    * NONE OF THIS REACHES THE READING.  Instructions at MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 are
      scheduling-invariant (CLAUDE.md §7); a foreign process on another core
      changes when the measured process runs, not how many instructions it
      retires.  The three-round medians confirm it: each arm's rounds agree to
      0.04–0.27 % while the effects the reading turns on are 1.5–1.7 % (interior)
      or are reported as being BELOW that spread (HS).
    * Nothing was signalled at any point.  Two processes were killed in this
      task and both were THIS AGENT'S OWN idle watcher shells, between batches,
      never a foreign process.

THE COUNTER CHECK — THE ONE THING THAT COULD HAVE STOPPED THIS LEG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An attached observer must change no trajectory.  Every attached/unattached pair
is compared by `scripts/compare_replay.py` (the committed comparator) with NO
`--residual-gate`: EXACT STRING EQUALITY on every column but `wall_s`, so
counters, statuses AND residuals must all be byte-identical.  The HS schema,
which that tool does not know, is compared by `comparator.py`'s own column walk,
which excludes the wall columns, the `trace` label and the `ev_*` per-site event
counts (those exist only when a sink is attached and ARE the evidence it fired)
and compares every remaining column exactly.

RESULT: 0 differences, every pair, every leg.  The gate did not need relaxing
and `--residual-gate` was never used.  Full listing in `comparator.out`.
