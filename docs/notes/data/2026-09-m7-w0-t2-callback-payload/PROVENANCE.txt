  * ONE SOLVE AT A TIME, alone on the box.  A `pgrep -af
    'cmake|ninja|ctest|hven_|bench_corpus'` is taken in a SEPARATE command
    before the lock and AGAIN inside it; both land verbatim in the batch's
    retained log as PGREP_EMPTY / PGREP_NONEMPTY.  EVERY BATCH OF THIS LEG
    RECORDED PGREP_EMPTY ON BOTH.  No process was declared-skipped.
  * A SECOND LANE SHARES THIS BOX.  A private benchmark-harness lane
    (~/Projects/hven-bench, M7 W1) ran cells during this task's window.  It was
    never signalled and never waited on beyond the lock: the in-lock pgrep is
    the record, and it is EMPTY for every batch here.  One of its cells was
    observed running BETWEEN batches (after the first interior batch closed at
    04:24:50Z and before the second opened at 04:26:16Z); the next batch was
    started only after a separate pgrep read clean.
  * `flock /tmp/box-build.lock` held across the whole batch, with
    FOREGROUND_START / FOREGROUND_END written INSIDE the lock.
  * MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 in every measured process.
  * `taskset -c 2` on the measured process; its SMT sibling cpu10 is left idle
    and is MEASURED, not assumed.  The driving shell is pinned OFF both
    (HARNESS_MASK = 0,1,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,11,12,13,14,15).
  * `set -o pipefail` in every leg script, and every wrapper worst-folds: the
    leg's exit status is the WORST status any step returned and a later success
    never clears it.  Proven against stubbed failures INSIDE the lock —
    logs/14-fold-proof.log, six wrappers, every one non-zero.
  * Nothing of this leg is written under /tmp except the box lock — with the
    one declared exception that is the shipped runner's and not this task's:
    the SQP corpus leg's fork/exec deadline writes a per-cell row marker under

THE TWO POPULATIONS, AND WHY THERE ARE TWO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(1) THE INTERIOR READING is the whole leg in ONE process per arm per round.
(2) THE SQP READING runs each cell as ONE PROCESS (`--internal-run-one`), which
    is T8.9r's and W6 T6's perf shape: the public SQP leg forks and execs a
    child per cell, so a `perf stat` over it would count 27 forks, 27 execs and
    a parent poll loop beside the solve.  The three arms run BACK TO BACK ON
    EACH CELL, so an attached and an unattached reading of one cell are
    adjacent in time.  ONE ROUND, which is what the brief asks for on this side
    (the SQP corpus was FLAT at T6 and the question here is only whether the
    mask makes it worse).

THREE INTERIOR BATCHES, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BATCH 1's ROUND-1 UNATTACHED PROCESS READ +3.5 % over its own rounds 2 and 3
— the same first-process class the ownership doc §11.3 names and W6 T6's batch 1
hit, and the median of three discards it.  Batch 1 ALSO reads its two attached

BATCHES were taken (`ipmleg2`, `ipmleg3`), each behind its own untimed,

to within 0.03 percentage points.  NOTHING IS DISCARDED: all three are in
`raw/` and `perf/`, `reading.md` states all three, and the verdict rests on the
two that agree.
