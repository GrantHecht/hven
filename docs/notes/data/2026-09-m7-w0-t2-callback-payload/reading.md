# M7 W0 T2 — the reading

What `IterationPayload` bought, measured under M6 W6 T6's own protocol on the
leg T6 called MOVED. Every figure here is regenerated by `comparator.py` from
`raw/` and `perf/`; nothing is hand-transcribed. Read `PROVENANCE.txt` first —
it carries the §7 stamp, the arms, the serial terms and what this leg does not
measure.

## 1. The short version

1. **The mask removes the majority of the measured attached cost, and it
   removes exactly the thing T6 named.** On the whole 43-row interior leg, a
   cheap callback at `kAll` costs **+1.376 %/+1.401 %** instructions
   (reproducing T6's +1.46 %/+1.71 %); the same callback at `kScalars` costs
   **+0.588 %/+0.618 %**. The mask takes out **56–57 %** of the attached cost.
2. **`a-scalars` is NOT FLAT.** It lands between 0.5 % and 1 %. That is a
   finding, not a failure, and it is reported with its number rather than
   tuned away.
3. **What the mask removes is O(n), and is confirmed to be so.** An attribution
   leg over the leg's smallest and largest cell populations reads the saving at
   **0.159e6** instructions per event at an event-weighted mean `x` of 1 088 and
   **1.614e6** at 14 647 — a 10.2× rise for a 13.5× width. That is the payload
   preparation, and it scales with the problem exactly as T6's §4 said it does.
4. **What is left does NOT scale with the problem.** The residual reads
   **2.94e6** instructions per event at mean `x` = 1 088 and **2.54e6** at
   14 647. A 13.5× change in width moves it 14 % — downward. So no wider mask
   reaches it, and §5 says what the leg can and cannot name it as.
5. **T6's declared omission is closed, and the interior sink is FREE.**
   `a-sink` — 538 events, the corpus's own `CountingTraceSink` — reads
   **+0.043 %/+0.039 %**: **FLAT**.
6. **The SQP corpus stays FLAT with the mask.** Walk mode, one round, 27 cells:
   `a-all` corpus ratio **1.00013**, `a-scalars` **1.00000**, 0 of 27 cells
   outside 0.99–1.01 in either arm. **FLAT / FLAT**, as expected.
7. **Counters byte-identical in all 32 comparisons** (42 750 cell-comparisons,
   0 differences). A declared payload changed no trajectory, on either engine.

## 5. What is left, and what this leg can and cannot name it

`a-scalars` did not land FLAT, so what remains has to be sized and, as far as
the evidence allows, named. The attribution leg (`raw/attrib/`, `perf/attrib/`)
runs the leg over its SMALLEST and LARGEST cell populations, three arms, three
alternated rounds each, behind its own warm-up.

| population | rows | events | mean `x` per event | a00 | a-all | a-scalars |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| small (the five `n1000` cells + the leg's four fixed rows) | 19 | 290 | 1 088 | 5 264 575 978 | 1.17073 | 1.16198 |
| large (the five `n20000` cells + the same four) | 19 | 296 | 14 647 | 57 787 777 272 | 1.02129 | 1.01302 |

Per event, against `a00`:

| population | mean `x` | a-all delta/event | a-scalars delta/event | **what the mask removed** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| small | 1 088 | 3 099 450 | 2 940 498 | **158 952** |
| large | 14 647 | 4 156 931 | 2 542 848 | **1 614 083** |

**THE MASK REMOVES AN O(n) COST, CONFIRMED.** The saving rises 10.2× for a 13.5×
rise in problem width. That is the attached-only payload preparation T6's §4
identified — the scatter to declared width, the three unit maps, the folded
bound price, the two constraint blocks and the declared-diagnostics pass — and
the mask takes it out in proportion to the problem, which is the shape a remedy
for an O(n)-per-event cost has to have.

**THE RESIDUAL DOES NOT SCALE WITH THE PROBLEM.** 2.94e6 per event at `x` =
1 088 against 2.54e6 at `x` = 14 647: a 13.5× width change moves it by 14 %, and
*downward*. So it is not payload preparation, and no widening of
`IterationPayload` reaches it.

**IT IS NOT THE ATTACH, AND IT IS NOT THE PER-EVENT DISPATCH.** The `a-sink`
arm settles that on this same leg: an observer attached through a different
guard, invoked 538 times through a virtual call, costs **+0.04 %**. A guarded
attach plus a per-event indirect call is free at this instrument's resolution.

**WHAT THE LEG CAN NAME.** One piece of attached-only work sits OUTSIDE
`fire_iteration_event` and is therefore untouched by the mask:
`src/drivers/ipm_solver.cpp:4117–4119`,

```
if (this->iteration_callback_) {
    last_step_norm = (alpha * DXSL.head(this->primal_vars_)).lpNorm<Eigen::Infinity>();
}
```

computed once per committed step whenever a callback is installed. It is O(n),
and the mask cannot remove it without changing what `kScalars` MEANS, because
`step_norm` is one of the fields `kScalars` promises. Everything else remaining
on the `kScalars` path is O(1) per event — the null guard, the two mask reads,
the provenance and restoration reads, one `steady_clock::now()`, the
`IterationEvent` aggregate, the `std::function` invocation and the two
pending-apply calls.

**WHAT THE LEG CANNOT DO IS SPLIT THEM, AND IT SAYS SO.** Three facts refuse a
clean attribution and are stated rather than smoothed:

* an O(n) term would scale with `x` and the residual does not;
* the O(1) terms named above cannot arithmetically account for ~2.5–2.9 × 10⁶
  instructions per event — that is three to four orders of magnitude more than
  a clock read, an aggregate and an indirect call;
* the residual is not constant across the three populations either: 1.36e6 per
  event on the 43-row leg against 2.5–2.9e6 on the two 19-row subsets, and the
  43-row leg's row set contains both subsets' rows. A quantity that were a sum
  of non-negative per-row or per-event contributions could not behave that way,
  so **part of the residual is process-level** — allocator and page state that
  an attached arm's extra allocations shift, which MKL's address-sensitive
  kernels then retire differently.

Separating those needs an instrument this task deliberately does not build: a
code change to the measured binary, which would stop it being the binary the
commits land. **So the residual is REPORTED WITH ITS NUMBER and its
non-scaling, the one named candidate is recorded with its file and lines, and
the split is left open.**

## 7. The disposition

**D24 (M7-4) is NOT closed. The mask lands as the lever; the item stays
registered with the residual's number.**

1. **The remedy works and is the right shape.** `IterationPayload` removes the
   cost T6 identified, in proportion to the problem, and it removes **56–57 %**
   of the whole measured attached cost on the leg that moved. It is additive:
   the one-argument attach still means `kAll` and no consumer changed.
2. **`a-scalars` is +0.588 %/+0.618 %, outside the ±0.5 % bar.** Stated as the
   finding the brief's settler note anticipates for exactly this range, not
   tuned toward FLAT.
3. **The residual is named as far as the evidence goes and no further** — §5.
   The one named candidate inside hven's own code is the `step_norm`
   computation at `src/drivers/ipm_solver.cpp:4117–4119`, which is O(n),
   attached-only, and feeds a field `kScalars` promises. Registered for a
   follow-up that would have to change what a scalars-only consumer is
   promised, or compute it lazily.
4. **T6's declared omission is CLOSED.** The interior leg has a sink lever and
   the interior sink arm is **FLAT** (+0.043 %/+0.039 %). Nothing is registered
   from it.
5. **The SQP side is re-confirmed FLAT with the mask**, both arms, walk mode.
6. **The instruments land** — `HVEN_LEG_TRACE_SINK`,
   `HVEN_LEG_CALLBACK_PAYLOAD` and the corpus's `--callback count-scalars` —
   so the next reader can re-take any of this without writing an instrument
   first, which is the same thing T6's disposition item 5 left behind.

## 8. AMENDMENT 1 — the unattached path, as a commit pair

**Added after §1–§7 were written, by the P-SYM gate.** That gate asks whether
the UNATTACHED path's instruction stream moved between the task's start commit
(`d8ed6b28`) and its HEAD (`b8db4b98`). P-SYM answers it per symbol; this
answers it at runtime, which is the reading that matters.

Two binaries, built in **equal-length sibling worktrees** so `__FILE__` is the
same length in both arms, over the same 43-row leg with **nothing attached**,
three alternated rounds behind one untimed warm-up
(`raw/unattached/`, `perf/unattached/`, `logs/75-unattached-pair.log`):

| arm | rounds (instructions) | median | own spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| `old` (`d8ed6b28`) | 108 317 513 145 / 108 368 080 501 / 108 368 694 049 | 108 368 080 501 | 0.047 % |
| `new` (`b8db4b98`) | 106 216 125 723 / 106 138 822 532 / 106 146 440 748 | 106 146 440 748 | 0.073 % |

**ratio new/old = 0.97950 — the unattached path retires 2.05 % FEWER
instructions at HEAD.**

**IT IS NOT A SPEED-UP, AND IT IS NOT WORK.** Three things say so together:

1. **Nothing in the diff can reach the unattached path.** P-SYM
   (`logs/72f-psym.out`) classifies every library-object difference: the only
   two differing objects are `ipm_solver.cpp.o` and `sqp_solver.cpp.o`, and
   within them every differing symbol is either an EDITED function
   (`fire_iteration_event`, `apply_pending_iteration_callback`,
   `set_iteration_callback`, `enter_restoration`, `invoke_iteration_callback`),
   a CONSTRUCTOR that now initialises two more members, or a
   **member-displacement shift** — `mov %rax,0x950(%r14)` → `mov
   %rax,0x958(%r14)`, same mnemonic, same operands, same length, every offset
   past `iteration_callback_` moved by exactly **+8 bytes**. That is the
   M6 W5 T1 displacement class, not new code.
2. **The trajectory is byte-identical across the pair.**
   `scripts/compare_replay.py`, 43 cells × 30 columns, all three rounds:
   **0 differences** (`logs/76-unattached-pair-counters.log`, and the three
   comparisons in `comparator.out`'s §3). The same solve, the same counters,
   the same residuals — so the two binaries do the same WORK.
3. **The magnitude is the same class §5 already had to name.** These two
   binaries were also built at different absolute levels from the measurement
   binary (108.4e9 and 106.1e9 here against 104.5e9 for the arm binary in §2),
   which is a ~2–4 % build-to-build spread on a leg whose arms, WITHIN one
   binary, agree to 0.02–0.17 %.

**THE CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS ARTIFACT, STATED PLAINLY.** Every verdict in §2, §4
and §5 is a **within-binary** comparison — one build, one binary, arms that
differ only by a lever — and is unaffected. **Cross-binary instruction
comparisons on this leg carry a layout component of the order of 2 %**, which
is why §2 declines to read anything into this leg's absolute counts differing
from W6 T6's, and why §5 attributes part of the `a-scalars` residual to
process-level layout rather than to per-event work. A future reader comparing
two BUILDS on this leg should treat ±2 % as the floor until that component is
instrumented.

**P-SYM's own verdict on the library objects, for the record.** 142 objects
across all groups: 46 byte-identical, 76 differing within the accepted noise
class, 19 with unclassified differences. **Of the library (`hven`) group,
exactly two objects differ — `ipm_solver.cpp.o` and `sqp_solver.cpp.o` — which
is what the brief predicted**, and their per-symbol breakdown is 226 identical /
1 noise-only / 17 differing / 1 only-after (interior) and 292 identical / 7
differing / 1 only-before / 2 only-after (SQP). The only-before/only-after pair
on each side is the one-argument `set_iteration_callback` becoming the
two-argument one.
