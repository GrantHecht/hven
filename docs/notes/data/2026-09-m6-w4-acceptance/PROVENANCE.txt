THE SERIAL RULE, AS DECLARED (CLAUDE.md section 7)
  Every measurement leg ran SOLO -- one process alone on the machine, one leg at
  a time, each held under the box build lock (`flock /tmp/box-build.lock`) with
  `pgrep` verified clean before it started. Measurement legs (the six replay
  legs and the exemplar/census probe) additionally ran pinned with
  `taskset -c 2` at MKL_NUM_THREADS=1 and OMP_NUM_THREADS=1. NO CO-RUN PROTOCOL
  WAS USED and none is declared: nothing here was measured under contention.

  The SMT sibling of the pinned core (cpu10) was left idle and measured from
  /proc/stat across the replay window:

      six-leg U0 replay window (2026-09-05 06:04:36Z - 06:12:53Z) .. 0.1148 % busy

  NO WALL-CLOCK NUMBER IN THIS DIRECTORY IS ASSERTED. Counters, statuses and the
  stream's own line counts are the asserted currency; every `wall_s` column,
  every `_s` field in an `ipm.solve.end` line, and every suite duration is
  informational and is excluded from every comparison below (75 of the corpus
  schema's 76 columns are compared).
