THE READING, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
  LTO moves the residual columns in essentially every cell -- 76 / 77 / 74
  residual cell-columns across walk / ssn / ipm -- and moves NOTHING ELSE except
  two step-length floats in one cell.  No integer counter and no status or
  verdict string differs anywhere, on any engine.  The moves are small in
  ABSOLUTE terms (the largest floor-only difference is 3.765e-18) but NOT always
  small relatively: 21 residual cell-columns per engine sit outside the 1e-5
  RELATIVE tolerance and are admitted only by the 1e-13 absolute floor, the worst
  of them a 3.166e-02 relative move on `kkt_primal` between 1.151644575e-16 and
  1.189293199e-16.  That is the calibration fact worth keeping: at the 1e-16
  scale these residuals live at, the FLOOR is the gate, not the tolerance -- so
  the 1e-5 figure is not what is holding this comparison together, and a future
  reader must not infer from "within gate" that LTO perturbs residuals by less
  than 1e-5 relative.  It does not.
