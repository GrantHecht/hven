## 1. What changed and why

The independent review of `ff8a8f2c3` returned SHIP AFTER FIXES. Code head of the riders: `130baf690` (`git diff 22c9b6879 -- include src` is
comments and the one registry description string only; no non-comment production line moves, so no counter replay is re-run: the replay of
`DECLARATION-RF-1c.md` leg N stands for the unchanged code). The riders:

- `Rf1RestorationStall.ANewRunKeepsTheBestAndForgetsTheSamples` gains a case pinning the keeps-best half of `new_run()`: a best of 1.0, a new
  run, then six passes (each costing 100 against a window of 10) falling 5.0 -> 2.0 must be cut on the sixth with the best kept (every sample
  carries 1.0, which is no 10% fall against itself) and is not cut by a window that never saw the 1.0.
- `Rf1RestorationSeam.ARestartNeedsTheFloorsPassesOfItsOwn` now counts the RESTART's own phase rows (those after the first run's failed
  subproblem row) and asserts exactly `kRestorationStallMinPasses + 1` = 6 (the rule implies exactly six: five passes behind the reference, the
  sixth observation is the verdict). Measured by the author: 6 with the fix, 3 without the `new_run()` call (the first run's twelve passes
  already behind the reference). The old assertion counted all phase rows (19 with, 16 without) and would have been masked by a first-run change.
- New `Rf1RestorationSeam.AFallingPhaseSurvivesItsWindowByTheFallTest`: LinearRowModel's phase (h 20 -> 0) with a window of 1 factorization runs
  six rows and is not cut; the solve equals the solve under a window that never binds. The phase survives by the fall test: with the fall test
  removed (M3) it is cut on the sixth pass.
- Comments and the option description: restoration.h class contract restated, sqp_solver.cpp phase comment, sqp_options.h reflow, the registry
  description ("... and at least five passes; a window of 0 means after five passes"); `docs/options-reference.md` regenerated by the repo's
  generator (`cmake --build <dir> --target options-reference`).
- Nits (as r3 copies; the originals are unedited): `legs-rf1c/rf1c_check_r3.py` (train and pilot iterate the union of both arms' cells and fail on
  a cell missing from either) and `legs-rf1c/tu2_report_r3.py` (union, and THREW_OR_RC fails its verdict). Both are re-run on the existing
  evidence; the verdicts are expected unchanged (`PILOT_BARS_MET`, `TU2_NO_LOSS_NO_FALSE_CERT`).

## 3. Leg V: the NET3 claim, measured (R1 on the TU-2 cells)

`DECLARATION-RF-1c.md` section 1 says the NET3 restart "depends on" `new_run()` keeping the running best. That was asserted, not measured. Leg V
builds arm P = the committed head with mutation R1 as a local-only commit (`legs-rf1c/rf1c_r1probe.sh`: lane install and TU-2 harness binary at
that pin, hven-bench-tu2 `c3d176e`, same build setup as arms R and H), runs the 48 TU-2 cells of `DECLARATION-RF-1c.md` leg U on the four pinned
physical cores of one box lane (co-run protocol of CLAUDE.md section 7: one process per pinned physical core, `MKL_NUM_THREADS=1`; counters,
statuses, objectives only; a deviation is a candidate to be re-run alone before it is called one), and reports P against H and against R with
`tu2_report_r3.py`. No bar: the result is reported either way (zero movers P vs H would mean the claim is not borne out on these cells; movers
on NET3 would support it). Evidence in `gate-RF-1c/r1probe/`. The leg is run only if it fits under an hour on the lane.
