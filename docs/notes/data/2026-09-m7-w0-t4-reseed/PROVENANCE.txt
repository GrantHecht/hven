THE REPLAY'S BINARY STAMP, AND WHY THERE ARE TWO ARMS OF IT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The gate leg's replay arm produced CSVs stamped `# binary: 0be12ec390c5-dirty`,
which is NOT the tree they were produced from. `bench/CMakeLists.txt:85-90` names
this trap in its own comment: the stamp is captured at CONFIGURE time, so a tree
configured earlier and REBUILT later emits the stale string. The code under test
was the clean 39746705 the gate's step 1 had just rebuilt -- the header change
forces `bench_corpus.cpp.o` to recompile -- but the embedded string was `build/`'s
configure-time one.

The remedy that comment gives is to re-configure first, so the replay was RE-TAKEN
that way (raw/gates/replay-restamp.log): stamp `39746705438c`, schema 76,
`budget_table_hash 0x357aee91dee27391`. Both arms are kept and the re-take
additionally compares the two against each other. THE RE-TAKEN ARM IS THE
AUTHORITY; the stale-stamped one is retained rather than deleted because its
numbers are what the gate leg actually saw.
