       true_neg/ref_neg_scaled), dense-taken matrices: = 100 % per engine (expected 2575/2575, 2764/2764). s = 10, 100
       reported beside. A failure stops DN-1 with the matrix, both references and the D blocks.
A2-IPM / A2-SQP  matrices where Pardiso (unperturbed) matches the reference and the arm does not, union: = 0 per engine.
A3     every matrix the arm finds exactly singular is taken by the fallback, arm == kSparse, outcome and evidence
       field-for-field equal to the kNever replay of the same matrix: all (expected 25/25).
A4     literal agreement with unperturbed Pardiso at n <= 8, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, all, per engine: REPORTED.
A5     on every dense-taken matrix the arm's negative count = MKL dsytrf read by det/trace (mkl_bk_neg); against DN-0's
       dense column it differs on exactly the 14 LAKES matrices of design [E3d], by +6 each: all.
A6     dsytrf_rook column (negative count vs the reference, exactly-singular count, max |L|, replay residual, isolated
       instructions per factorization and solve): REPORTED.
A7     min |D-block eigenvalue| / max |a_ij| over dense-taken factorizations, per engine, decidable/undecidable: REPORTED.
A-thr  the arm's negative counts at the default thread setting: identical to MKL_NUM_THREADS=1, or the differences listed.
S1     both engines on DN-0's 238 cells, kAlways vs kNever on the same build: every kNever success (optimal or
       acceptable) keeps its status and objective within 1e-8*max(1,|f|). Not met -> reported per cell with its
       kSuspect-conversion trace; the settler rules.
S2     every kNever non-success cell's kAlways status reported; a move to success with objective and terminal KKT
       residuals; a move between two non-success statuses in the same detail. Not gating (owner-accepted, C266).
S3     reported per cell, both arms: iterations, factorizations, dense_factorize_count, dense_fallback_count (where the
       engine exposes them), inertia_suspect_verdicts, inertia_wrong_verdicts, suspect_escalations,
       eqp_residual_refusals, eqp_row_drift_refusals, border_uncertified_fallbacks, dep_inertia_rereads,
       dep_inertia_reread_flips, zero_motion_exhausted. Every move declared.
