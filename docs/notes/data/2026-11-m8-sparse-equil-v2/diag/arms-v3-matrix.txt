Reading:
- CRESC100.never is the PAIRING class (only arms with weighted matching win it); ALLINITC.never is the SCALE class (P or MS win
  it, M alone does not). STEENBRB qp3's certificate/factorization cost regression is caused by iparm[10] scaling (MS), not by
  stickiness. qp26's cycle is reached only under sticky matching.
- PM as implemented does NOT escalate where it must: on qp26 minor 15 and on CRESC100 the P retake still perturbs pivots
  (qp26: 5 perturbed, inertia 274/260, random-rhs probe eta 0.25) yet its refined answer to the EQP's actual rhs passes the
  equilibrated check (eta 1.07e-7) while the engine's own residual check REFUSES it (ratio 3.7e7). The walk then takes its
  ordinary refusal retreat, so PM degenerates to P. The escalation criterion must count an engine refusal (and/or perturbed
  pivots surviving the P retake) as a P failure.
