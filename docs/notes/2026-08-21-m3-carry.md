## 1. Measurement policy (owner rulings, made during phase C)

- **The census is a gate/event instrument post-M3, not a per-change one**
  (owner, 2026-08-19, folded in phase-C plan §6). Per-change proof ladder:
  byte-identity (P-SYM/PCH tooling) for mechanical changes; suite + bench
  counters for behavior-preserving work; declared re-derivation for
  intentional numeric change. Census runs at: milestone gates, flag or
  toolchain events, releases, on-demand when the cheap legs disagree, and
  optionally on a weekly schedule.
- **Reduced-budget census variant (OPTION, not yet ruled):** the DNF tier
  burns ~2 h proving "still doesn't converge in 3600 s". A tripwire variant
  running the DNF tier at a reduced declared budget cannot confirm the
  budget-relative status pins but serves as a cheap full-coverage tripwire
  (~3.5 h total); the full-budget run stays the gate instrument. Decide
  post-M3 if census cadence makes it worth maintaining two protocols.
- **Layout-band runtime-neutrality standard** (owner, 2026-08-17, amended
  2026-08-18): counters bit-identical unconditional; pooled median +
  engine-heavy cells judged with a +0.5 % reproducible-slowdown threshold;
  sub-second cells carry a one-sided +1.5 % cost band. Calibrated on the
  T-series' three independent draws (layout jitter oscillates, does not
  accumulate). Reuse for any future TU/layout-affecting change.

## 2. Flags and toolchain

- **LTO is OFF everywhere we measure** (opt-in `LINK_TIME_OPT`; forced on
  only in the unexercised wheel path). Turning it on anywhere quotable is a
  U0-class declared flag event: every flag-sensitive pin, the census
  baseline, and the bench arms re-derive. The wheel path needs that
  treatment before anything from it is ever quoted.
- **CI lanes pin a fixed ISA** (`HVEN_SIMD_ARCH`: x86-64-v3 Linux/Windows,
  apple-m1 macOS); local/derivation regime is `native`. Ruled at U0
  (design note 2026-08-16 §2). Do not "clean up" the cache variable.
- **PCH membership bar**: faster AND byte-identical. Every SQP-side TU
  measured so far (6 for 6) fails byte-identity — the measurement table in
  src/CMakeLists.txt is the record; predictions are banned (0-for-5), only
  measurements.
